I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [Instituto Superior Técnico](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/), Universidade de Lisboa (Lisbon, Portugal), co-advised by Professor [Joana Campos](https://gaips.inesc-id.pt/team/joana-campos/) and Dr. [Filipa Correia](https://iti.larsys.pt/member/filipa-correia/). I am a researcher at [GAIPS](https://gaips.inesc-id.pt/) lab in [INESC-ID](https://www.inesc-id.pt/), where I also contribute to scientific outreach and social media communication. I hold an MSc in Biomedical Engineering from [TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/) (Austria) and a BSc in Biomedical Engineering from IST (Lisbon), and I currently teach undergraduate courses in Computer Science.

My research sits at the intersection of **human–robot interaction** and **assistive technologies** — I design intelligent systems, from social robot interactions to serious games, that support people with cognitive impairments. I am particularly drawn to executive functions such as sustained attention, working memory, and cognitive flexibility. At the core of my work is a commitment to moving beyond deficit-focused approaches: I want to build technology that genuinely empowers people and respects their autonomy.
