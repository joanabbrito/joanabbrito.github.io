Lisbon, Portugal

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa (Lisbon, Portugal), co-advised by Professor Joana Campos and Dr. Filipa Correia. I am a researcher at GAIPS lab in INESC-ID, where I also contribute to scientific outreach by handling the social media communication of the lab. I hold an MSc in Biomedical Engineering from TU Wien (Austria) and a BSc in Biomedical Engineering from IST (Lisbon), and I currently teach undergraduate courses in Computer Science.

My research sits at the intersection of human–robot interaction and assistive technologies — I design intelligent systems, from social robot interactions to serious games, that support people with cognitive impairments. I am particularly drawn to executive functions such as sustained attention, working memory, and cognitive flexibility, and to how technology can help develop and support them — a question I got to explore in depth through my involvement in the EMPOWER EU project. In my work I would like to move beyond deficit-focused approaches: I want to build technology that genuinely empowers people and improves their sense of autonomy.
